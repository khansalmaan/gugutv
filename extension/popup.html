How it works

  1. Open a supported video site in each participant's browser.
  2. One person picks a Room ID and shares it; everyone else enters it here and clicks Join room.
  3. Play, pause and seek then sync automatically for everyone in the room.
  4. If playback ever looks stuck or out of sync (e.g. right after an ad), click the ⟳ button to re-attach.
